This release adds a formally verified coordinate-swap analysis connecting the ordered pairs (2,3) and (3,2). A Lean 4 proof defines coordinate exchange tau(a,b)=(b,a), proves tau(2,3)=(3,2), tau(3,2)=(2,3), tau²=I, and proves that (2,3) and (3,2) remain distinct ordered pairs while belonging to the same two-point swap orbit. The Lean layer intentionally contains no semantic constants for songs, authors, memory states, or physics claims; it proves only explicit pair algebra.

The release also adds a creative-corpus background analysis. Physics, determinism, geometry, E8, triality, cybernetics, and sonification are treated as recurring parts of Trent Slade’s declared creative environment rather than as independently surprising lyric features. Vector Equilibrium, Superdeterminism, Memory Virus, and Silicon Ziggurat are recorded as the author-reported principal physics-related album corpus. Public Memory Virus album existence is kept separate from first-party claims about its SPECTRAL production lineage. The author also reports that the earlier EP Sonification 2005 does not itself contain sonification-related lyrics or sound; SAW-1 uses that author-reported premise as a negative-control reminder that a technical-looking title does not by itself establish technical content.

An earlier Cybernetic God motif is recorded as an author-reported candidate parse of (2,3), pending transcript-level archival verification. If that candidate is later verified, the Lean-proved coordinate-swap theorem applies mathematically to the parsed pair. SAW-1 does not claim that the theorem proves lyric provenance or explains how the later lyric was produced.

The release additionally documents ordinary cognitive and creative possibilities relevant to motif recurrence, including implicit-memory effects, source monitoring, source-attribution uncertainty, and pattern completion as general research background. Repository-defined language such as “associative reconstruction with uncertain source attribution” is explicitly separated from literature-established terminology. “Cryptomnesia-like self-retrieval” is treated only as a qualified analogy, not as a plagiarism claim or a literature-established mechanism for reuse of one’s own work. SAW-1 makes no diagnosis of photographic memory, eidetic memory, source-monitoring disorder, exceptional memory, or any neurological mechanism.

Authorial-intent boundaries are also recorded. God Is a DJ is identified by the author as a creative influence, including on Quasicrystal Shocks; “His bassline is law” is interpreted by the author against a long-running deterministic and superdeterministic vocabulary; and the word “God” is intentionally left open to interpretation rather than being assigned a required religious or theological meaning.

The overall conclusion remains deliberately conservative. SAW-1 documents a structured semantic and mathematical correspondence, a bounded creative-corpus context, and ordinary explanatory possibilities. It does not claim prophecy, retrocausality, paranormal information transfer, physical entanglement between creative artifacts, proof of a second time dimension, a reconstructed psychological history, or causal exclusivity.
